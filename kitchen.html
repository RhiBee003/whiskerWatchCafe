Seeded rosemary oat crackers

House cracker for the charcuterie board — oats, seeds, olive oil, rosemary. Crisp · whole-grain · no added sugar. Yield ~36 (~4 board shares) · bake 350°F · 18–24 min.

~35–40 kcal each · ~1.5 g protein · ~1 g fiber · 0 g added sugar

Ingredients

  • 1 cup rolled oats (½ cup blitzed coarse · ½ cup whole)
  • ½ cup almond flour
  • 3 tbsp ground flaxseed
  • 2 tbsp sesame · 2 tbsp sunflower · 1 tbsp chia optional
  • 1 tsp minced rosemary (or ½ tsp dried)
  • ½ tsp flaky sea salt (+ more to finish)
  • ¼ tsp black pepper · ¼ tsp garlic powder optional
  • 3 tbsp extra-virgin olive oil
  • ¼ cup water (plus 1–2 tbsp if dry)
  • 1 tbsp plain Greek yogurt

Directions

  1. Heat oven to 350°F. Line two sheets with parchment.
  2. Pulse ½ cup oats to coarse flour; keep ½ cup whole.
  3. Mix oat flour, whole oats, almond flour, flax, seeds, rosemary, salt, pepper, garlic.
  4. Stir in olive oil, Greek yogurt, and water until dough holds when pressed. Rest 5 min.
  5. Roll very thin between parchment (~⅛" / 2–3 mm).
  6. Score into squares. Sprinkle flaky salt.
  7. Bake 18–24 min until deep golden (rotate halfway). Optional: oven off, door cracked 5–8 min for extra snap.
  8. Cool completely on the sheet; break along scores.

Blueberry Greek Yogurt Muffin

Banana-sweetened batter with oat–almond crumble — no sugary streusel. Yield 12 · bake 350°F · 18–23 min.

~115–125 kcal · ~5 g protein · 0 g added sugar

Batter

  • 1½ cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 cup plain Greek yogurt
  • 2 very ripe bananas, mashed
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 cup blueberries
  • ¼ cup unsweetened almond milk · 1 tsp vanilla
  • 1½ tsp baking powder · ½ tsp baking soda · ½ tsp cinnamon · pinch salt
  • Optional: ¼ cup PB Fit (reduce flour slightly)

Crumble (12 muffins)

  • ¼ cup rolled oats · 2 tbsp oat or AP flour
  • 1 tbsp almond butter · 1 tbsp chopped almonds
  • ½ tsp cinnamon · pinch salt · 1–2 tsp almond milk as needed

Directions

  1. Heat oven to 350°F; prep a 12-cup tin.
  2. Mix wet (banana, yogurt, eggs, milk, vanilla); fold into dry until just combined.
  3. Fold in blueberries. Divide among cups.
  4. Mix crumble to small crumbs (not paste); ~1½–2 tsp per muffin.
  5. Bake 18–23 min until toothpick is clean or moist crumbs.

Asiago Bagel

Boil-then-bake bagel with Greek yogurt dough and real Asiago in + on top. Yield 8 · bake 425°F · 18–22 min.

~190–220 kcal · 11–13 g protein · 0 g added sugar

Dough

  • 2 cups AP flour · ½ cup white whole-wheat (or 2½ cups AP)
  • 1 cup nonfat Greek yogurt · 1 egg
  • 2 tsp instant yeast · 1 tsp salt · ½ tsp garlic powder optional
  • ¾ cup shredded Asiago (½ cup in dough · ¼ cup topping)
  • 2 tbsp warm water as needed

Boil bath

  • 8 cups water · 1 tbsp baking soda
  • Skip malt/honey for 0 added sugar

Directions

  1. Mix dough with ½ cup Asiago; knead 6–8 min. Rise until doubled.
  2. Shape 8 rings; rest 10–15 min.
  3. Boil 30–60 sec per side; drain.
  4. Egg wash; top with remaining Asiago.
  5. Bake 425°F · 18–22 min until deep golden.

Banana PB Protein Cookie

Soft Greek-yogurt cookie — roasted banana forward, light bake, finished with a healthy cocoa drizzle (no chocolate chips · no added sugar). Yield 12 · roast bananas 400°F · bake 375°F · 10–13 min.

~100–115 kcal · ~5 g protein · 0 g added sugar · cocoa drizzle

Cookie

  • 2 very ripe bananas (or 2½ medium) — roast first
  • ½ cup plain Greek yogurt (2% preferred)
  • 1 large egg
  • ¼ cup + 1 tbsp PB Fit
  • 1 cup all-purpose flour
  • 3 tbsp unsweetened almond milk
  • ¾–1 tsp baking powder · ¼ tsp baking soda
  • ½ tsp vanilla · ¼ tsp cinnamon · pinch salt
  • Optional: ¼–½ tsp banana extract · 1 tbsp melted butter

Cocoa drizzle (0 added sugar)

  • 2 tbsp unsweetened cocoa powder
  • 1 tbsp PB Fit
  • 1–2 tbsp unsweetened almond milk
  • 1 tsp mashed roasted banana (optional)
  • Tiny pinch salt · optional ¼ tsp vanilla

Directions

  1. Roast peeled bananas 400°F · 12–15 min until syrupy; mash.
  2. Heat oven to 375°F; line a sheet with parchment.
  3. Mix banana, yogurt, lightly whisked egg, vanilla, milk, optional extract + butter.
  4. Stir in PB Fit, flour, baking powder, baking soda, cinnamon, salt — just until combined.
  5. Scoop 12; flatten to ⅓ inch. Bake 10–13 min until edges set, centers soft.
  6. Cool 10–15 min. Whisk drizzle; zigzag over cookies. Let set 5–10 min.

Chocolate Protein Croissant

Yeast dough with Greek yogurt — not a laminated butter croissant. Cocoa + PB Fit filling. Yield 8 · bake 375°F · 15–20 min.

~180–210 kcal · 9–11 g protein · 2–3 g fiber

Dough

  • 2 cups AP flour · ¾ cup Greek yogurt · 1 egg
  • 2 tbsp almond milk · 1 tbsp melted butter
  • 1 tsp instant yeast · ½ tsp salt
  • Optional 1 tbsp honey (omit for 0 added sugar)

Filling

  • ¼ cup PB Fit or chocolate protein · 1 tbsp cocoa
  • 2 tbsp Greek yogurt · 1–2 tsp almond milk
  • Optional honey or mashed banana

Directions

  1. Mix dough; knead smooth. Rise ~1 hour.
  2. Roll rectangle; cut 8 triangles.
  3. Spread filling at wide end; roll to point; curve ends.
  4. Rise 30–45 min on parchment. Egg wash.
  5. Bake 375°F · 15–20 min until golden.
