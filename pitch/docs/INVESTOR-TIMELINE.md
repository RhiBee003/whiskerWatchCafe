## What to show them — order of materials

Use this sequence every time (Zoom or in-person). Don’t dump everything at once.

### 1. Hook (60–90 seconds) — mouth only, then one visual
**Say:** “I’m opening a rescue-partnered cat lounge + specialty café at Jade in North Hyde Park — glass-front bar, sealed lounge, patio — **Caffe Vita** Seattle beans only at the bar because I grew up there and the coffee is that good — and a weekly Girlz and Gears bike night to fill Wednesdays.”

**Show:** Live café site hero (or the portfolio café screenshot).  
→ https://rhibee003.github.io/whiskerWatchCafe/

### 2. The site (2–3 minutes) — make it real
**Show:**
1. Jade Google Maps / street context  
2. ACRE listing page  
3. Floor-plan schematic from the pitch (DBPR split: bar | lounge | BOH | patio)

**Say:** Suite 2 · 1,666 SF interior + 895 SF patio · 42 retail garage stalls · under 192 apartments · UT / Riverwalk / downtown.

### 3. Why it’s legal / operable (2 minutes) — kill the fear early
**Show:** Pitch section *Health-code plan (Florida / DBPR)*

**Say:**
- Cats never enter food prep.
- Packaged snacks + drinks **can** go into the lounge; prep stays café-side.
- Same dual-zone model Florida cafés already run.
- Confirm with Hillsborough / DBPR at plan review.

### 4. How money comes in (2–3 minutes)
**Show:** Pitch *Unit economics* (Y1 ramp + Y2 mix + ask)

**Say:** Sessions (**$22 / guest**, weekday early from $20) + café bar (**Caffe Vita** · lattes/frappes · healthy sweets) + merch/events. Wednesday bike night = midweek patio tabs + optional lounge blocks. Base case ~$433K Y1 / ~$532K Y2; EBITDA re-checked after rent quote.

### 5. The ask + use of funds (2 minutes)
**Show:** *$280K use of funds* table

**Lead with:** Finish-out / glass+HVAC split ($90K) + 4 months working capital incl. cat care ($78K). Everything else is equipment, deposits, launch.

### 6. Risks (1–2 minutes) — you say them first
**Show:** *Risks unique to Jade*

Name landlord animal consent + rent as #1 and #2. Then your mitigations. Credibility > polish.

### 7. Soft close / next step
**Ask for one thing:**
- Intro to someone who does Tampa F&B / real-estate angel checks, **or**
- Soft circle pending rent quote + landlord letter, **or**
- Follow-up meeting with the full pitch canvas open together.

**Leave behind (optional PDF/link pack):**
1. Café site  
2. Investor pitch canvas export / this folder’s pitch notes  
3. ACRE Jade link  
4. One-page ask (raise, use of funds, timeline to open)

**Do not open first:** full Spotify playlist, cat photo filler gallery, WhiskerWatch app deep-dive, or code repos — those are flavor after they’re hooked.
