# Whisker Watch — Protection Plan
**Keep the business and idea yours before showing anyone**

---

## Core truth
You cannot permanently lock an “idea.” You protect the **company, brand, confidential numbers, and control**.

---

## 1. Form the company first
- File **Whisker Watch LLC** (Florida) in your name  
- Get EIN; open business bank account  
- Operating Agreement: you = **Managing Member**, you own **100%** until a raise  
- Optional: USPTO trademark search/file for “Whisker Watch” (café / pet services)  

Do **not** pitch as if investors are buying into a formed company until the LLC exists.

---

## 2. What to share vs hold

| Safe early | Hold until serious interest |
|---|---|
| Concept, mission, neighborhood-level site | Exact suite #, broker emails, full model |
| High-level market | Line-by-line build budget |
| Rough raise ($280K) | Cap table wishes, rescue names |
| That dual-zone model exists | Full 3D as “construction docs” |

First contact = **teaser only** (`02-teaser-and-founder-terms.md`).

---

## 3. How to talk to people
- **Brokers / landlords:** dual-zone animal-lounge concept; not the full investor deck  
- **Rescues:** exploratory first; MOU after LLC  
- **Investors:** many won’t sign an NDA on first look — share teaser; watermark full pitch later  
- **“Helpers”:** treat like investors — teaser or NDA  

Watermark: `Confidential – Whisker Watch LLC – Do Not Distribute`

---

## 4. Paper that keeps it yours
Florida small-business attorney:
1. Operating Agreement (founder control)  
2. Short NDA (contractors, designers, advisors)  
3. IP assignment (“work product belongs to LLC”)  
4. Term sheet (you stay Managing Member; narrow investor vetoes)  

---

## 5. Sequence
1. LLC + OA + EIN  
2. Trademark search if name is final  
3. One-pager teaser  
4. Soft Colliers ask: Rome Collective suite + rent + animal lounge allowed? (no full pitch blast)  
5. Rescue exploratory call  
6. Full pitch to vetted investors only (after site rough-in)  

---

## 6. Red lines — walk away
- They want **>49%** for $280K with no ops role  
- They want **Managing Member** rights day one  
- They want the **idea assigned to them** before money hits the LLC  
- Anyone asking to shop the deck without you  

---

## Bottom line
Stay founder and owner by **owning the LLC first**, sharing a **teaser not the crown jewels**, and selling only **minority equity** with **you as Managing Member**.

---

*Not legal advice. Finalize with a Florida attorney.*
