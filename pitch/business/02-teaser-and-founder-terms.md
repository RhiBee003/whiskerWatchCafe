## Whisker Watch Café — Confidential Teaser

**Whisker Watch Café** is a reservation-led rescue cat lounge and specialty café in Tampa’s urban core — espresso and light bites in a health-code-compliant dual-zone space, with adoptable cats in a separate lounge.

| | |
|---|---|
| Seed raise target | **~$280,000** |
| First unit | 1 café (Tampa) |
| Model | Founder-led operator |

### The opportunity
Tampa has strong visitor and pet-spend demand and limited cat-café supply. Whisker Watch pairs a proven dual-zone operating model (café bar separated from cat lounge) with a North Hyde Park–area mixed-use ground-floor concept — walkable to students, downtown, and daytime professional traffic.

### What we’re building
- Timed lounge sessions + café attach revenue  
- Rescue partnership (adoption-first; café is display/foster site)  
- Target colony: **10–12 cats** on the floor + quarantine capacity  
- **Wednesday Girls Bike Nights** with **Girlz and Gears** (patio hang after the ride)  
- Memberships, private events, and merch as upside  

### The ask
Raising approximately **$280,000** in seed capital for buildout, working capital, and launch of the first location. **Founder remains operator and majority owner** under a founder-control structure.

### What we share next (on request)
Full confidential pitch: site details, unit economics, colony care budget, use of funds, and health-code plan.

# PART B — Founder-control term sheet outline
**Illustrative · Have a Florida attorney finalize**

Goal: you stay **founder**, **Managing Member**, and **majority owner**.

| Term | Founder-friendly position |
|---|---|
| Issuer | Whisker Watch LLC (Florida) |
| Security | Membership interest (equity) — or SAFE/convertible if preferred |
| Raise amount | $280,000 (may close in tranches) |
| Investor ownership (illustrative) | **20–35%** post-money for $280K |
| Founder ownership | **Majority (65–80%)** retained |
| Management | Founder = **sole Managing Member** |
| Board / managers | Managing Member decides ops; optional informal advisors only |
| Investor veto (narrow) | Only: selling the company; issuing new equity; debt above $X; related-party deals |
| No veto on | Hiring, menu, vendors, marketing, rescue partners, session pricing |
| Founder draw | Modest owner draw after break-even or as budgeted |
| Information rights | Quarterly P&L + bank summary; annual K-1 |
| Non-compete | Investors: no competing Tampa cat café for 24 months using Whisker Watch materials |
| IP & brand | All IP/brand/work product owned by the **LLC** |
| Transfer restrictions | Investor cannot transfer without Managing Member consent (ROFR) |
| Drag / tag | Tag-along for minority; drag only with founder consent or >75% vote |
| Use of proceeds | Buildout, deposits, equipment, cat setup, working capital, launch |
| Conditions to close | LLC formed; Operating Agreement signed; funds to **LLC bank account only** |

### Hard no’s
- Investor gets **>49%** for this raise  
- Investor becomes Managing Member  
- Idea/IP assigned to investor personally  
- Unlimited dilution without founder consent  
- Right to “shop” the deal without you  

### Docs your attorney should produce
1. Operating Agreement (founder control)  
2. Subscription / membership purchase agreement  
3. Short NDA (contractors & advisors)  
4. IP assignment for designers/devs  
5. Final term sheet matching this outline  

### One-liner
> “I’m raising $280K for a minority stake. I remain Managing Member and majority owner; investors get economics and narrow veto on sale, new equity, and major debt.”
