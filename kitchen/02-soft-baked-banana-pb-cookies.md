# Soft-baked banana peanut butter Greek yogurt cookies

**Café name idea:** Soft-Baked Banana PB Greek Yogurt Cookies · **Banana PB Protein Cookie** ($5)  
**Yield:** 12 cookies  
**Bake:** **375°F · 10–13 min**  
**Profile:** Soft bakery cookie · less dense · Greek yogurt + PB Fit  
**Related oat batch:** [01-banana-pb-cookies.md](01-banana-pb-cookies.md)

Café **default / signature** formula — lighter texture baked in.

---

## Ingredients (batch of 12)

- 2 bananas (~350 g total mashed)  
- ½ cup plain Greek yogurt *(2% preferred over nonfat; or 6 tbsp yogurt + extra milk)*  
- 1 large egg  
- **⅓ cup PB Fit**  
- **1 cup** all-purpose flour  
- **3 tbsp** unsweetened almond milk  
- **¾–1 tsp baking powder**  
- **¼ tsp baking soda**  
- 1 tsp vanilla  
- ½ tsp cinnamon  
- Pinch of salt  
- **Optional:** 1 tbsp melted butter (bakery-soft crumb)  

---

## Directions

1. Heat oven to **375°F**. Line a sheet with parchment.  
2. Mash bananas well; mix in yogurt, lightly whisked egg, vanilla, almond milk, and optional butter.  
3. Stir in PB Fit, flour, baking powder, baking soda, cinnamon, and salt **just until combined** — don’t overmix.  
4. Scoop **12** cookies; flatten to **⅓ inch**.  
5. Bake **10–13 minutes** until edges set and centers still soft.  
6. Cool on pan **10–15 minutes**.
