# Soft-baked banana peanut butter Greek yogurt cookies

**Café name idea:** Soft-Baked Banana PB Greek Yogurt Cookies  
**Yield:** 12 cookies  
**Profile:** Soft bakery cookie · egg + AP flour · marketable ~100 kcal snack  
**Related:** Oat-only base is [01-banana-pb-cookies.md](01-banana-pb-cookies.md)

Scaled from per-cookie notes in the shared album.

---

## Ingredients (batch of 12)

- 2 bananas (~350 g total mashed)  
- ½ cup plain Greek yogurt  
- 1 large egg  
- ½ cup PB Fit  
- 1 cup + 2 tbsp all-purpose flour  
- 2 tbsp unsweetened almond milk  
- ½ tsp baking powder  
- 1 tsp vanilla  
- ½ tsp cinnamon  
- Pinch of salt  

---

## Directions

1. Heat oven to **350°F**. Line a sheet with parchment.  
2. Mash bananas well; mix in yogurt, egg, vanilla, and almond milk.  
3. Stir in PB Fit, flour, baking powder, cinnamon, and salt until thick and scoopable.  
4. Scoop **12** cookies; flatten slightly.  
5. Bake **12–16 minutes** (or **14–17** if dough was wetter) until edges set.  
6. Cool on pan **10–15 minutes**.
