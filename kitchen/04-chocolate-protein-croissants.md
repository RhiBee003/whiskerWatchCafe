# Healthier chocolate protein croissants

**Café name idea:** Chocolate Protein Croissants  
**Yield:** 8  
**Bake:** 375°F · 15–20 min  
**Profile:** Yeast dough with Greek yogurt · cocoa / PB Fit filling · lighter than laminated croissant

Not a true laminated croissant — healthier shortcut with rise time.

---

## Dough

- 2 cups all-purpose flour  
- ¾ cup plain Greek yogurt  
- 1 egg  
- 2 tbsp unsweetened plain almond milk  
- 1 tbsp honey or maple (optional — omit for no added sugar)  
- 1 tsp instant yeast  
- ½ tsp salt  
- 1 tbsp melted butter  

## Chocolate filling

- ¼ cup PB Fit or chocolate protein powder  
- 1 tbsp unsweetened cocoa powder  
- 2 tbsp Greek yogurt  
- 1–2 tsp almond milk  
- 1–2 tsp honey **or** mashed banana (optional)  

**Best low-sugar filling tip:** unsweetened cocoa + PB Fit + a little banana — chocolate–PB richness without a sugary bar.

## Egg wash

- 1 egg beaten with 1 tsp almond milk  

---

## Directions

1. Mix flour, yeast, and salt.  
2. Add Greek yogurt, egg, almond milk, and melted butter (and honey if using).  
3. Knead until smooth and soft.  
4. Cover; rise **~1 hour**.  
5. Roll into a large rectangle.  
6. Cut into **8 long triangles**.  
7. Mix filling until thick and spreadable.  
8. Put a small amount of filling at the wide end of each triangle.  
9. Roll toward the point; curve ends slightly.  
10. Place on parchment; rise **30–45 minutes**.  
11. Brush with egg wash.  
12. Bake at **375°F** for **15–20 minutes** until golden brown.
