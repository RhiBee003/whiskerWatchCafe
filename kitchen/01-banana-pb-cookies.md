# Banana PB Fit Greek-yogurt cookies (oat flour)

**Café name idea:** Soft Banana PB Breakfast Cookies  
**Yield:** 10–12 soft cookies  
**Bake:** 350°F · 12–16 min  
**Profile:** No added sugar · soft / scoopable · banana + peanut butter

---

## Ingredients

- 2 very ripe bananas  
- ½ cup plain Greek yogurt  
- ½ cup PB Fit powder  
- 1 cup oat flour  
- ½ tsp baking powder  
- 1 tsp vanilla extract  
- ½ tsp cinnamon  
- Pinch of salt  
- **Optional:** 1–2 tbsp milk (or almond milk) if dough is too thick  

**Flavor upgrade:** Roast very brown bananas at **400°F ~15 min** before mashing for stronger banana vs PB Fit.

---

## Directions

1. Heat oven to **350°F**. Line a baking sheet with parchment.  
2. Mash bananas extremely well (no large lumps). Microwave ~30 sec if not soft enough; thaw + drain if frozen.  
3. Mix in Greek yogurt and vanilla.  
4. Add PB Fit, oat flour, baking powder, cinnamon, and salt.  
5. Stir to a **thick, scoopable dough**. Let sit **5 minutes** so oat flour hydrates.  
6. Scoop **10–12** portions; flatten to ~½ inch.  
7. Bake **12–16 minutes** until edges are set and tops look slightly dry.  
8. Cool on the pan **10 minutes** (they firm as they cool).

**Texture tip:** If dough was too wet, add a little more oat flour and aim sticky-not-pourable; bake closer to **14–17 min**.
