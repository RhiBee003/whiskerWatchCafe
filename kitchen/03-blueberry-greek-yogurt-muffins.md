# Soft-baked blueberry Greek yogurt muffins

**Café name idea:** Soft-Baked Blueberry Greek Yogurt Muffins  
**Yield:** 12 muffins  
**Bake:** 350°F · 18–23 min (toothpick clean / moist crumbs)  
**Profile:** Banana-sweetened · no added sugar in batter · optional oat–almond crumble

---

## Muffin batter

- 1½ cups all-purpose flour  
- 1 cup plain Greek yogurt  
- 2 very ripe bananas, mashed  
- 2 eggs  
- 1 cup fresh or frozen blueberries  
- ¼ cup plain unsweetened almond milk  
- 1 tsp vanilla extract  
- 1½ tsp baking powder  
- ½ tsp baking soda  
- ½ tsp cinnamon  
- Pinch of salt  

**Optional protein:** ¼ cup PB Fit (or other protein powder) — reduce flour slightly (protein absorbs moisture).

---

## Healthy crumble topping (12 muffins)

Oat + almond butter · no added sugar:

- ¼ cup rolled oats  
- 2 tbsp oat flour or AP flour  
- 1 tbsp almond butter  
- 1 tbsp finely chopped almonds  
- ½ tsp cinnamon  
- Pinch of salt  
- 1–2 tsp unsweetened almond milk (only as needed)

**Mix** with fingers into **small crumbly pieces** (not a paste).  
**Top** ~1½–2 tsp per muffin before baking.

**Starbucks-style crumbly vibe:** oats + almond flour + almond butter + cinnamon (good on blueberry).

---

## Directions

1. Heat oven to **350°F**. Line or grease a 12-cup muffin tin.  
2. Mash bananas; whisk with yogurt, eggs, almond milk, and vanilla.  
3. Whisk dry ingredients; fold into wet until just combined.  
4. Fold in blueberries (don’t overmix).  
5. Divide batter among 12 cups.  
6. Mix crumble; sprinkle on top.  
7. Bake **18–23 minutes** until toothpick comes out clean or with moist crumbs.  
8. Cool in pan a few minutes, then on a rack.
