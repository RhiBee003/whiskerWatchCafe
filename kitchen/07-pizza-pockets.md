# Healthy pizza pockets

**Café name idea:** Garden Pizza Pocket  
**Yield:** 8 pockets  
**Bake:** **400°F · 16–20 min**  
**Profile:** Savory · Greek yogurt dough · veggie-heavy · light cheese · no added sugar  

Hand-held café savory — pizza flavor without a greasy pastry shell.

---

## Dough

- 2 cups all-purpose flour *(or 1½ cups AP + ½ cup white whole-wheat)*  
- 1 cup plain nonfat or 2% Greek yogurt  
- 1 large egg  
- 1 tsp instant yeast  
- ¾ tsp salt  
- ½ tsp garlic powder  
- ½ tsp Italian seasoning  
- 1–2 tbsp warm water if dough is stiff  
- 1 tsp olive oil *(optional · softer crust)*  

## Filling (split across 8)

- ⅔ cup no-sugar-added marinara or crushed tomatoes + pinch salt, oregano, garlic  
- ¾ cup part-skim mozzarella, shredded  
- 2 tbsp grated Parmesan  
- ½ cup finely chopped mushrooms  
- ½ cup finely chopped spinach or baby kale (squeezed dry if wet)  
- ¼ cup finely diced bell pepper  
- 2 tbsp minced onion or shallot  
- Optional lean: 2–3 tbsp chopped turkey pepperoni or cooked chicken  
- Black pepper · pinch red pepper flakes  
- Optional: 2 tbsp low-fat ricotta *(creamier center)*  

## Finish

- 1 egg white + 1 tsp water (egg wash)  
- Pinch Italian seasoning or sesame on top  

---

## Directions

1. Mix flour, yeast, salt, garlic powder, and Italian seasoning. Stir in Greek yogurt, egg, and optional oil. Add water by the teaspoon until a soft dough forms.  
2. Knead **4–5 minutes** until smooth. Cover; rest **20–30 minutes** (or rise up to 45 min).  
3. Sauté mushrooms + onion **3–4 minutes** until moisture cooks off; cool. Mix with spinach, pepper, cheeses, marinara (don’t drown — filling should be thick), pepper flakes, and optional turkey/chicken.  
4. Heat oven to **400°F**. Line a sheet with parchment.  
5. Divide dough into **8** balls. Roll each into a ~5–6" oval.  
6. Spoon **~2 tbsp** filling on one half; leave a ½" border. Fold over; crimp with a fork. Poke 2 small steam vents on top.  
7. Egg wash; dust with Italian seasoning.  
8. Bake **16–20 minutes** until deep golden. Rest **5 minutes** before serving (filling is hot).

**Make-ahead:** Assemble, freeze on a sheet, bag. Bake from frozen **+4–6 min**.  
**Café serve:** warm, with a side cup of no-sugar marinara (~2 tbsp) if requested.
