# Healthy Asiago bagels

**Café name idea:** Toasted Asiago Bagels  
**Yield:** 8 bagels  
**Bake:** boil 1 min/side → **425°F · 18–22 min**  
**Profile:** Chewy bagel · Greek yogurt dough · Asiago in + on top · no added sugar

Lighter than a classic bakery Asiago bagel (less butter/oil in dough; protein from yogurt + cheese).

---

## Dough

- 2 cups all-purpose flour (plus more for dusting)  
- ½ cup white whole-wheat flour *(or use 2½ cups AP total)*  
- 1 cup plain nonfat Greek yogurt  
- 1 large egg  
- 2 tbsp warm water (or unsweetened almond milk), as needed  
- 2 tsp instant yeast  
- 1 tsp salt  
- ½ tsp garlic powder *(optional, pairs with Asiago)*  
- ¾ cup shredded Asiago cheese, divided  
  - **½ cup** into the dough  
  - **¼ cup** for topping  

## Boil bath

- 8 cups water  
- 1 tbsp baking soda *(chewy crust)*  
- Optional: 1 tsp honey or barley malt *(classic shine — skip for 0 added sugar)*  

## Finish

- 1 egg white + 1 tsp water (egg wash)  
- Remaining Asiago for tops  
- Optional: cracked black pepper  

---

## Directions

1. Mix flours, yeast, salt, and garlic powder.  
2. Stir in Greek yogurt, egg, and **½ cup Asiago**. Add warm water 1 tbsp at a time until a soft, slightly tacky dough forms.  
3. Knead **6–8 minutes** until smooth and elastic.  
4. Cover; rise until roughly doubled (**45–75 min**).  
5. Divide into **8** equal pieces. Roll each into a ball, poke a hole in the center, and stretch into a bagel ring (~2–2½" hole — it shrinks).  
6. Rest shaped bagels **10–15 minutes** while you bring the boil bath to a gentle boil.  
7. Boil **30–60 seconds per side**. Lift out; drain on a rack or towel.  
8. Brush with egg wash. Top with remaining Asiago (+ pepper if using).  
9. Bake at **425°F** for **18–22 minutes** until deep golden and cheese is browned.  
10. Cool on a rack at least **15 minutes** before slicing.

**Make-ahead:** Shape, freeze on a sheet, then bag. Boil from frozen (+15–30 sec) and bake.

**Café serve:** Warm split with light cream cheese or whipped cottage cheese; or toast and serve beside Vita espresso.
