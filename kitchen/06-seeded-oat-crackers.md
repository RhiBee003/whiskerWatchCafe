# Seeded rosemary oat crackers

**Café name idea:** Seeded Oat Crackers *(for Cheese & fruit board)*  
**Yield:** ~36 thin crackers (~4 board shares of 8–9)  
**Bake:** **350°F · 18–24 min** (rotate once)  
**Profile:** Crisp · whole-grain · seed-forward · no added sugar · made for charcuterie

House cracker for the **Cheese & fruit** patio board — pairs with soft cheese, fruit, and light cured meats without a heavy boxed cracker.

---

## Ingredients

- 1 cup rolled oats (blitz half to coarse flour, leave half whole for texture)  
- ½ cup almond flour *(or finely ground almonds)*  
- 3 tbsp ground flaxseed  
- 2 tbsp sesame seeds  
- 2 tbsp sunflower seeds (chopped if large)  
- 1 tbsp chia seeds *(optional · extra crunch)*  
- 1 tsp fresh rosemary, minced *(or ½ tsp dried)*  
- ½ tsp flaky sea salt (+ more to finish)  
- ¼ tsp black pepper  
- ¼ tsp garlic powder *(optional)*  
- 3 tbsp extra-virgin olive oil  
- ¼ cup water *(plus 1–2 tbsp if dough is dry)*  
- 1 tbsp plain Greek yogurt *(binds · adds a little protein)*  

---

## Directions

1. Heat oven to **350°F**. Line two sheets with parchment.  
2. Pulse **½ cup** of the oats to a coarse flour; keep the other **½ cup** whole.  
3. In a bowl, mix oat flour, whole oats, almond flour, flax, sesame, sunflower, chia, rosemary, salt, pepper, and garlic powder.  
4. Stir in olive oil, Greek yogurt, and water until a soft dough holds when pressed (add water by the teaspoon if crumbly).  
5. Rest **5 minutes** so flax/chia gel.  
6. Place dough between two sheets of parchment. Roll **very thin** (~⅛" / 2–3 mm).  
7. Peel top parchment. Score into squares or rectangles with a knife or pizza wheel. Sprinkle a pinch of flaky salt.  
8. Bake **18–24 minutes** until deep golden at the edges (rotate sheets halfway). For extra snap, turn oven off and leave crackers inside **5–8 minutes** with the door cracked.  
9. Cool completely on the sheet — they crisp as they cool. Break along score lines.

**Storage:** Airtight tin up to **5 days** (or freeze). Recrisp 3–4 min at 325°F if soft.

**Board build (Cheese & fruit for two · $15):**  
soft cheese + hard cheese · seasonal fruit · handful of olives or nuts · **8–10 crackers** · optional thin cured meat.
