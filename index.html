The visit

Designed as two clean zones

Florida health code keeps cats out of food prep. Jade’s floor plate gives us a street-facing bar, a glass-separated lounge, and an outdoor patio — the same dual-zone model that works in Tampa.

Café bar

Espresso, pour-overs, and packaged bites at a quartz counter. Humans only — patio seating when you want the street.

Cat lounge

Timed sessions with 10–12 adoptable cats behind glass. Vertical wood shelves, quiet corners, capped guest counts.

The colony

Adoptable by design

We partner with local rescue so every lounge cat is looking for a home. Sessions stay capped — calm for the colony, better for guests.

  • 10–12 cats on the lounge floor
  • Age 8+ in the lounge
  • 12–15 guests per session
  • Rescue-owned — café is foster & display
